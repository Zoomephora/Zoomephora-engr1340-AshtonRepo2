Find the numbers: Identify the dividend (the big number being split up) and the divisor (the number you are dividing by). 

Use multiplication facts: Think of the multiplication table for your divisor. For example, if the problem is 15 ÷ 3, think: "What times 3 equals 15?" 

Write the answer: Write down the matching number. Since \(3 \times \mathbf{5} = 15\), your answer (quotient) is 5. 

Check for leftovers: If the number doesn't fit perfectly, whatever is left over is your remainder. For example, 16 ÷ 3 is 5 with a remainder of 1.
